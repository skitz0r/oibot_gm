# Loot policy (MOCK, for the prototype)

_This is a stand-in written for the demo. The real guild's policy is whatever
their officers say it is; replace this file with it._

1. **Mainspec before offspec.** Offspec only when no mainspec candidate has the
   item at tier A or better.
2. **Bigger upgrades first.** Prefer the candidate for whom the item is the
   larger upgrade (S+ > S > A > B), using the guild's tiering.
3. **Attendance matters.** Between comparable upgrades, prefer the raider with
   higher attendance over the last 30 days. Someone under 50% attendance
   should not beat a regular for an S-tier item.
4. **Spread the wealth.** Recent loot counts against you: a raider who has
   received two or more S-tier items in the last two weeks yields to one who
   hasn't, unless the upgrade gap is a full tier.
5. **Tanks and healers are not penalised for offspec farming gear**, but
   their tank/heal mainspec items come first.
6. **Alts and trials** sit behind mains and raiders for S/S+ items. A trial
   may receive A-tier items freely.
7. **Weapons and trinkets** (the big slots) weigh more than jewellery when
   counting "recent loot".
8. **Unregistered characters** (signups we can't match to a known raider)
   are treated as trials until an officer confirms them.
9. **Ties go to the longer wishlist wait.** If still tied, the council rolls.
